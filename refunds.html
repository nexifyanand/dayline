Refund & Cancellation Policy

Rules applicable to purchases, cancellations, and refunds.

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the rules applicable to purchases, premium plans, cancellations, refunds, and account termination for Dayline.

Dayline is developed and operated by Nexifylabs as an independent developer.

By purchasing a Dayline plan, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this policy.

1. Paid Plans

Dayline may offer paid plans that provide access to premium features or other benefits. The available plans, prices, duration, and features will be clearly displayed before you complete a purchase.

Current plans may include:

  • Monthly Plan — ₹49/month
  • Annual Plan — ₹299/year
  • Lifetime Plan — ₹799 one-time payment

Plans, prices, features, and availability may change in the future. The terms applicable to your purchase are those displayed to you at the time of purchase, subject to applicable law.

2. No General Refund Policy

All Dayline purchases are final.

Except where a refund is required by applicable law or where Dayline confirms that a transaction was incorrectly processed, Dayline does not offer refunds for completed purchases. This includes purchases made because of:

  • Change of mind.
  • Accidental purchase.
  • Lack of use of the premium features.
  • Failure to use the purchased plan.
  • Not liking the premium features.
  • Purchasing the wrong plan.
  • Purchasing a plan and later deciding not to use Dayline.
  • Forgetting that a purchase was made.
  • Device replacement.
  • Changing or losing access to an account.
  • Account suspension or termination resulting from a violation of the Dayline Terms & Conditions.

Please review the plan details and price carefully before completing your purchase.

3. No Cancellation After Purchase

Once a paid purchase has been successfully completed, the purchase cannot ordinarily be cancelled.

For plans that provide access for a defined period, the purchased entitlement remains subject to the applicable plan terms until its stated expiry or termination. Cancellation of an account does not automatically result in a refund of a previously completed purchase.

4. No Automatic Renewal

Unless expressly stated otherwise at the time of purchase, Dayline plans do not automatically renew.

When a plan does not include automatic renewal, no additional payment will be charged for the next period unless you voluntarily make another purchase. If automatic renewal is introduced for a particular plan in the future, the applicable renewal terms will be clearly disclosed before or at the time of purchase.

5. Account Suspension or Ban After Purchase

Purchasing a Dayline plan does not give you an unrestricted or permanent right to use the Service. Your use of Dayline remains subject to the General Terms & Conditions.

If your account is suspended, restricted, or permanently terminated because you:

  • Violate the General Terms & Conditions.
  • Engage in fraudulent activity.
  • Attempt unauthorized access.
  • Abuse or attack the Service.
  • Circumvent payment or security systems.
  • Attempt to evade a previous ban.
  • Use Dayline for unlawful purposes.
  • Repeatedly or seriously violate Dayline policies.

Your purchased premium access may be suspended or permanently revoked.

No refund, credit, transfer, extension, or compensation will ordinarily be provided for unused premium access resulting from an account suspension or termination caused by your violation of the Terms. This does not limit any rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

6. Purchased Plans Are Non-Transferable

Dayline purchases are linked to the account used to make the purchase.

Purchased plans, premium entitlements, and lifetime access are personal to the purchasing account and may not be:

  • Sold.
  • Gifted.
  • Transferred.
  • Assigned.
  • Exchanged for another account.
  • Converted into cash.
  • Shared with another person.

If you lose access to your account because of circumstances within your responsibility, the purchase does not automatically transfer to another account.

7. Lifetime Plan

If Dayline offers a Lifetime Plan, "lifetime" refers to the lifetime of the applicable Dayline product/service and does not mean a guaranteed lifetime of the purchaser or an unconditional guarantee that the Service will operate indefinitely.

A Lifetime Plan provides the benefits specifically described at the time of purchase, subject to the applicable Terms & Conditions. A Lifetime Plan does not:

  • Guarantee that every future Dayline feature will be included.
  • Guarantee access to discontinued or replaced features.
  • Make the purchase transferable.
  • Override account suspension or termination resulting from a violation of the Terms.

If the Dayline Service is permanently discontinued, any rights or remedies will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

8. Duplicate or Erroneous Charges

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or that a transaction was processed incorrectly, please contact Dayline support as soon as possible. We will review the transaction.

If we confirm that a duplicate or erroneous charge occurred, we may issue an appropriate correction or refund, subject to the circumstances and applicable law.

9. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a payment was made without your authorization, contact us promptly and provide the relevant transaction information. We may request information necessary to investigate the transaction. Unauthorized transactions may also need to be reported to the relevant payment provider or financial institution.

You should never provide Dayline with your:

  • UPI PIN.
  • ATM PIN.
  • Card PIN.
  • Banking password.
  • One-time password (OTP).

Dayline support will never require these credentials to process a refund or investigate a payment.

10. Payment Gateway and Third-Party Processing

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers. The payment provider may have its own terms, policies, and procedures relating to payment processing.

A successful payment does not override Dayline's Terms & Conditions or this Refund & Cancellation Policy. Where a payment provider requires a different procedure for a specific transaction issue, you may also be required to follow the provider's applicable process.

11. Taxes and Additional Charges

Applicable taxes or charges, where required, may be included in or added to the displayed purchase price. The final amount payable will be displayed before you confirm the purchase where applicable.

12. Promotional Offers and Discounts

Dayline may occasionally provide promotional offers, discounts, or special pricing. Unless specifically stated otherwise, promotional purchases remain subject to this Refund & Cancellation Policy.

A promotional price does not create an automatic right to a refund or exchange. Promotional offers may have additional conditions that will be displayed at the time of the offer.

13. Account Deletion After Purchase

Deleting your Dayline account does not automatically create a right to a refund.

If you voluntarily delete your account after purchasing a plan, the purchase remains non-refundable except where a refund is required by applicable law or an erroneous transaction is confirmed.

Account deletion and data handling are governed separately by the Dayline Data Security & Deletion Policy and Privacy Policy.

14. Account Ban and Purchased Benefits

If an account is permanently banned because of a serious or repeated violation of the Dayline Terms & Conditions, the associated premium entitlement may be permanently revoked.

A purchased plan does not prevent Dayline from suspending or terminating an account for violations of the Terms. Unused time, unused premium features, or unused Lifetime Plan benefits resulting from such termination will not ordinarily be refunded, transferred, converted into another account, or settled for cash.

This provision is subject to any mandatory rights available to the user under applicable law.

15. Exceptions Required by Law

Although Dayline generally does not offer refunds or cancellations after a completed purchase, nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict a refund, cancellation, replacement, or other consumer right that is legally required and cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Where applicable law provides a mandatory remedy, Dayline will comply with that requirement.

16. How to Contact Us About a Payment

If you believe there is a genuine payment problem, duplicate charge, erroneous transaction, or unauthorized transaction, contact us through the official Dayline support channel.

Please provide, where available:

  • Dayline account email.
  • Transaction/order ID.
  • Purchase date.
  • Purchased plan.
  • Amount charged.
  • Relevant payment information that does not contain sensitive payment credentials.

Do not send your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, password, or OTP.

We may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy when our plans, payment systems, business practices, or applicable legal requirements change. The "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this policy indicates when it was most recently revised.

For significant changes, we may provide reasonable notice through the Dayline application, website, or another appropriate method.
