Data Security & Deletion Policy

How we protect your data and handle account deletion.

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

At Dayline, we understand that your journal may contain deeply personal information. This Data Security & Deletion Policy explains how we protect account information and User Content, how you can request access, export, correction, or deletion of your information, and what happens to your data when an account is deleted, suspended, or terminated.

Dayline is developed and operated by Nexifylabs as an independent developer.

This Policy should be read together with the Dayline Privacy Policy and General Terms & Conditions.

1. Our Approach to Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Dayline against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse, or destruction.

Because no digital service can guarantee absolute security, we cannot promise that information will always remain completely secure.

Our security practices are designed to reduce risks and protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

2. Types of Information Covered

This Policy may apply to information associated with your Dayline account, including:

  • Account information.
  • Authentication information.
  • Journal entries and User Content.
  • Images or attachments where supported.
  • Subscription and transaction-related records.
  • Technical and diagnostic information.
  • Security and authentication logs.
  • Information submitted when requesting support or exercising privacy rights.

The exact information stored depends on the features you use.

3. Security Measures

Depending on the type of information and the technical architecture of Dayline, security measures may include:

3.1 Secure Communication

Where supported, information transmitted between the Dayline application, website, and servers is protected using appropriate secure communication technologies.

3.2 Access Controls

Access to personal information is restricted to authorized systems, personnel, or service providers who require access for legitimate operational purposes.

3.3 Authentication Protection

We use reasonable measures to protect account authentication and reduce the risk of unauthorized account access.

3.4 Infrastructure Security

Systems used to operate Dayline may include security controls designed to protect servers, databases, storage systems, and other infrastructure.

3.5 Monitoring and Logging

Security and technical logs may be maintained to detect suspicious activity, investigate incidents, troubleshoot problems, and maintain the reliability of the Service.

3.6 Backups

Where backups are used, they may be protected using appropriate security measures and retained only for operational, disaster-recovery, or other legitimate purposes.

Backup copies may not be deleted immediately when you delete individual information or your account because backup systems may operate separately from the primary Service.

4. Access to Journal Content

Your journal content is intended to remain private.

Access to your User Content is limited to what is reasonably necessary to provide the features you request, maintain the Service, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, or comply with applicable law.

Dayline does not sell your journal entries or intentionally provide them to advertisers for advertising purposes.

Third-party infrastructure providers may process information on Dayline's behalf where necessary to provide hosting, storage, authentication, security, payment, or other technical services.

5. Your Responsibility for Account Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your Dayline account and device.

You should:

  • Keep your login credentials confidential.
  • Use a strong password where passwords are supported.
  • Avoid sharing your account with other people.
  • Keep your device and operating system reasonably secure.
  • Use official versions of the Dayline application.
  • Avoid entering account credentials into suspicious websites or applications.
  • Contact Dayline if you suspect unauthorized access.

Dayline support will never ask you to provide your password, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, card PIN, CVV, or one-time authentication code.

6. Security Incidents and Data Breaches

We take potential security incidents seriously.

If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will investigate the incident and take reasonable steps to contain, assess, and protect affected information, and notify affected users or authorities where required by applicable law.

7. Security of Third-Party Services

Dayline may rely on trusted third-party service providers for functions such as cloud hosting, database storage, authentication, payment processing, email delivery, crash reporting, and security infrastructure.

Third-party providers have their own security and privacy policies.

8. Data Storage and Location

Dayline may store personal information using infrastructure operated by Dayline or its third-party service providers. Depending on the services used, information may be stored or processed in India or other jurisdictions in compliance with applicable requirements.

— DATA ACCESS, EXPORT & DELETION —

9. Access to Your Information

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about personal data associated with your Dayline account, including categories of personal information, processing purposes, and relevant third-party processing.

We may need to verify your identity or account ownership before fulfilling certain requests.

10. Correcting Your Information

If information associated with your account is inaccurate or incomplete, you may request correction or updating where applicable using in-app controls or by contacting support.

11. Data Export

Where an export facility is available, you may be able to export certain information associated with your account, such as journal entries, notes, and account information.

If you intend to delete your account, we recommend exporting information you wish to keep before submitting the deletion request.

12. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Dayline account. An account deletion request results in the permanent deletion of your account, profile information, journal entries, and associated User Content.

Account deletion is generally permanent and may not be reversible.

13. How Account Deletion Works

When you request account deletion, Dayline follows a secure process to verify the request, disable the account, delete or anonymize eligible information from active systems, and process backup copies according to backup-retention procedures.

14. Data Remaining After Account Deletion

Deleting an account does not necessarily mean that every record is immediately erased from every system. Certain information may need to be retained temporarily or longer where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, financial records, fraud prevention, security investigations, or resolving disputes.

15. Backup Data

Deletion from active systems may occur before corresponding backup copies expire. Backup copies will be deleted or overwritten according to the applicable backup-retention process.

16. Deletion of Individual Journal Entries

Where Dayline provides deletion functionality for individual journal entries, you may delete individual entries directly through the application.

17. Deletion of Images and Attachments

Deleting relevant content or your account initiates the deletion of those files from active systems, subject to temporary retention in backups for security and reliability.

— ACCOUNT SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION —

18. Data When an Account Is Suspended

If an account is temporarily suspended, information may remain stored while the suspension is active to investigate violations, maintain security records, and protect other users.

19. Data When an Account Is Permanently Banned

If an account is permanently terminated because of a violation of the Terms & Conditions, we may restrict access and retain necessary information for security, fraud prevention, investigation, and legal compliance.

20. Banned Accounts and Data Requests

A permanent account ban does not automatically mean that all associated information is immediately deleted. Users may still submit valid requests concerning their personal information subject to applicable law.

21. Data Retention

Dayline retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law.

  • Account information: Retained while the account remains active.
  • Journal content: Retained while necessary or until deleted by the user.
  • Transaction records: Retained for accounting, legal, and tax purposes.
  • Security logs: Retained for security and operational purposes.

22. Legal Retention Requirements

Some information may be retained after deletion requests where required by law, such as tax/accounting requirements, payment records, or legal claims.

23. Privacy Rights and Deletion Requests

Subject to applicable law, you have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.

24. How to Request Data Deletion

To request account or data deletion, use the deletion facility within Dayline or contact us via our official support channel.

25. Requests Made by Another Person

We do not delete accounts or provide account information based solely on requests from unauthorized third parties without proper verification.

26. What Happens to Premium Plans After Deletion

Deleting your Dayline account does not automatically create a refund entitlement. Purchased plans are subject to our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

27. Data Security After Deletion

Removed information is no longer accessible via your account, while legally or technically retained records remain under strict confidentiality and protection.

28. Children's Data

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child improperly, we will take appropriate steps to delete it where required.

29. Changes to Security Practices

This Policy may be updated when our security, deletion, retention, or data-management practices materially change.

30. No Absolute Security Guarantee

No application, database, or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. You acknowledge that security risks can never be completely eliminated.

31. Relationship With Other Dayline Policies

This Policy should be read together with the General Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Refund & Cancellation Policy.
